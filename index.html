COLD STEEL · RELEASE NOTES

VERSION 1.35.00 · OUR UPDATE

  • Overhauled Ironvale Canyon & Airfield with true heightfield terrain, sculpted valley rim, canal & 3 bridges, and industrial fuel depots.
  • Made roads solid 3D objects, removed tan terrain mounds, and aligned all structures & hitboxes to terrain elevation.
  • Fixed vehicle terrain following for tanks, SAM trucks, APCs, and helicopters, plus added enemy base anti-spawncamp defense.
  • Converted on-foot aiming (ADS) to a toggle, added USA vs USSR team selection, and added Soviet-inspired T-80, Mi-24 Hind, 2K12 Kub, and BTR-80 models for USSR.

VERSION 1.1.10 · COMBAT AI & MODEL OVERHAUL

  • Enemy infantry now head for an available team vehicle as soon as they spawn or respawn.
  • Expanded bot patrol lanes to an 8 × 8 grid covering the full battlefield.
  • Rebuilt infantry and refreshed vehicle, terrain, building, tree, rock, and supply models.

VERSION 1.3.00 · BATTLE ROYALE & ARSENAL EXPANSION

  • Added Battle Royale mode: 3rd-person C-17 deployment, unarmed drop, parachute & ground weapon crates, 2.5-minute looting peace, and Players Remaining HUD.
  • Added 4 new weapons: SCAR-H Battle Rifle, P90 PDW, AA-12 Auto Shotgun, and Desert Eagle .50.
  • Added persistent burning vehicle wrecks with temporary terrain collision.

VERSION 1.2.00 · PUBLIC MATCHMAKING & HUD

  • Public queues show the operator roster and automatically start matches after a short countdown.
  • Vehicle HUD ammunition is split into clear side-by-side readouts for both weapon systems.
  • Fixed rooftop helipads raising players who are still beneath the platform.

VERSION 1.1.5 · BATTLEFIELD EXPANSION

  • Added rooftop Apache pads, stair access, covered base structures, and perimeter lighting.
  • Improved vehicle detailing and expanded rolling terrain across the map.
  • Bot patrol routes now sweep a wider grid of battlefield sectors.
  • Vehicle destruction and the cinematic intro now feature enhanced explosions and camera work.
