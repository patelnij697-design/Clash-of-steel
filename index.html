CLASH OF STEEL

WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump · LMB fire · RMB hold = scope · R reload · 1/2 or Q = primary/secondary · TAB = loadout (unlock guns by leveling up) · E enter/exit the jeep or helicopter
APC: armored, W/S drive, A/D steer, mouse aims the mounted MG (LMB), runs enemies over. Tank: mouse aims the turret, LMB cannon. Helicopter: W/S fly, A/D turn, Space/Shift climb/descend, LMB chain gun, F missile (homes on locked enemy helis).
Enemy tanks and helicopters shrug off bullets — use the AT Rocket (5) on tanks and the AA Missile (6) on helis.
